# Conventional CD4 T cell

## Definition

Helper CD4-positive T cell that is not of the regulatory lineage.

## Markers

- CD40LG is expressed after activation and separates conventional cells from the
  regulatory population in the fixture evidence.
- FOXP3 is absent, which is the negative side of the same comparison.

Related marker pages: [CD40LG](../marker_genes/CD40LG.md).
