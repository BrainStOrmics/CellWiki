# Fixture source (external note, paraphrase only)

The surface marker IL2RA (also written CD25) is highly expressed on regulatory T
cells and is recorded in the fixture evidence alongside the lineage factor
FOXP3. The note repeats that a single marker is not enough to call a cell
regulatory without activation context.
