# Monocyte

> Circulating myeloid cells that can infiltrate tumors and differentiate into macrophages or dendritic cells.

## Markers

| Marker | Type | Evidence | Source |
|--------|------|----------|--------|
| CD45 | positive | human scRNA-seq data collection | [exmaple_paper_2]() |

## Functional Characteristics

- **Precursors to macrophages and dendritic cells** (myelopoiesis)
  - Source: [exmaple_paper_2]()
- **Decrease in proportion in Spp1-KO tumors** (immune cell trafficking)
  - Source: [exmaple_paper_2]()
- **Serve as circulating precursors that differentiate into tissue-resident and tumor-associated macrophages** (Myelopoiesis)
  - Source: [exmaple_paper_2]()
- **Circulating myeloid precursors to tumor-associated macrophages** (myelopoiesis)
  - Source: [exmaple_paper_2]()
- **Circulate in blood and differentiate into tissue macrophages or dendritic cells** (myeloid differentiation)
  - Source: [exmaple_paper_2]()
