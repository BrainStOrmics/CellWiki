# Bone Marrow Derived Macrophage

> Macrophages differentiated from bone marrow progenitors in vitro, used to assess direct tumor cell interactions.

## Functional Characteristics

- **In vitro differentiated macrophages used to study direct effects on cancer cell proliferation** (macrophage differentiation)
  - Source: [exmaple_paper_2]()
- **Used for adoptive transfer and cell therapy to enhance ICB efficacy** (immunotherapy)
  - Source: [exmaple_paper_2]()
- **Model in vitro TAM responses to interferons and cytokines** (macrophage polarization)
  - Source: [exmaple_paper_2]()

  - Source: [exmaple_paper_2]()

  - Source: [exmaple_paper_2]()
