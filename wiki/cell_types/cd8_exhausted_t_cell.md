## Functional Characteristics

- **High clonal expansion and proliferation with retained anti-tumour effector potential** (T cell exhaustion and proliferation)
  - Source: [exmaple_paper_1]()
- **Exhibits features of in vivo exhaustion under chronic stimulation** (T cell exhaustion)
  - Source: [exmaple_paper_1]()
- **IGFLR1 synergizes with TCR signaling to upregulate HAVCR2** (Co-stimulatory signaling)
  - Source: [exmaple_paper_1]()
- **Chronic antigen exposure leading to functional impairment and high proliferative capacity** (T cell exhaustion, Proliferation)
  - Source: [exmaple_paper_1]()
- **Exhibits exhaustion-related gene expression and reduced IFNγ production** (T cell exhaustion pathway)
  - Source: [exmaple_paper_1]()
- **Impaired effector function due to chronic antigen stimulation** (T cell exhaustion pathway)
  - Source: [exmaple_paper_1]()
