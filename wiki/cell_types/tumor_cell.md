# Tumor Cell

> Malignant cells that evade immune responses and interact with stromal/immune compartments.

## Markers

| Marker | Type | Evidence | Source |
|--------|------|----------|--------|
| Cd45 | negative | flow cytometry sorting (DAPI-CD45-) | [exmaple_paper_2]() |

## Functional Characteristics

- **Can lose IFN-γ pathway genes to resist immunotherapy; interact with immune cells to shape microenvironment** (IFN-γ signaling pathway)
  - Source: [exmaple_paper_2]()
- **Form solid tumors and interact with TAMs to shape the microenvironment** (tumor-stroma crosstalk)
  - Source: [exmaple_paper_2]()
