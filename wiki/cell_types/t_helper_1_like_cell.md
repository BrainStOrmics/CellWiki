# T Helper 1 Like Cell

> A clonally expanded, tumor-enriched CD4+ T cell subset characterized by CXCL13 and BHLHE40 expression, prevalent in MSI colorectal cancers.

## Functional Characteristics

- **Clonally expanded and highly proliferative in MSI tumors** (T cell receptor signaling)
  - Source: [exmaple_paper_1]()
- **Regulates IFNγ production and represses IL-10 via BHLHE40** (Cytokine signaling)
  - Source: [exmaple_paper_1]()
- **Associated with favorable response to anti-CTLA4 immunotherapy** (Immune checkpoint response)
  - Source: [exmaple_paper_1]()
