# SPP1 Positive Tumor Associated Macrophage

> A tumor-enriched macrophage subset characterized by high SPP1 expression that drives immunosuppression and resistance to immune checkpoint blockade.

## Markers

| Marker | Type | Evidence | Source |
|--------|------|----------|--------|
| SPP1 | positive | scRNA-seq clustering and flow cytometry | [exmaple_paper_2]() |

## Functional Characteristics

- **Promotes tumor progression, angiogenesis, and immunotherapy resistance by dampening IFN-γ-STAT1-ISG signaling** (SPP1-SOCS1 pathway, angiogenesis, TGF-β production)
  - Source: [exmaple_paper_2]()
