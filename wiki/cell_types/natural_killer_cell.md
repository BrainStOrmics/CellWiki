> Innate immune lymphocytes identified in the tumor microenvironment.

## Functional Characteristics

- **Innate lymphoid cells involved in tumor surveillance** (cytotoxicity)
  - Source: [exmaple_paper_2]()
- **Increase in proportion in Spp1-KO tumors and contribute to innate anti-tumor responses** (innate cytotoxicity)
  - Source: [exmaple_paper_2]()
- **Provide innate cytotoxic surveillance against tumor cells** (NK cell receptor signaling)
  - Source: [exmaple_paper_2]()
